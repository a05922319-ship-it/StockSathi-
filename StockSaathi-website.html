A win for both sides

Hyperlocal, not warehouse

StockSaathi aims to connect shoppers with existing neighbourhood shops instead of replacing them.

🛍️

For shoppers

  • Reduce wasted trips
  • Check item availability before leaving
  • Discover nearby local shops
  • Free access to search and browse
🏪

For shopkeepers

  • Get discovered by nearby customers
  • Update stock with a simple dashboard
  • Use existing phones — no expensive hardware needed
  • Optional paid plan and promotions in the proposed model
Proposed business model

Fair monetisation for thriving kiranas

As described in your concept PDF, StockSaathi may earn through monthly shopkeeper plans, optional shop promotions, and a small commission per confirmed sale. Shoppers remain free.

These are proposed revenue streams, not active payments or confirmed earnings.

Our journey

Start local. Grow thoughtfully.

01 · Research: understand kirana pain points.

02 · Prototype: build a simple stock-sync experience.

03 · Local pilot: test with a small group of neighbourhood shops.

04 · Area launch: expand after inventory accuracy and shop adoption improve.
